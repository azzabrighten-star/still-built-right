Privacy Policy: still-built-right

Last updated: November 20, 2026

What the app is

"still-built-right" is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Still Built Right to read that channel's own analytics. It is not a public service.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the app uses two read-only Google scopes:

  • youtube.readonly: the channel's list of videos and their titles
  • yt-analytics.readonly: the channel's aggregated analytics (views, watch time, traffic sources, countries)

The app cannot upload, edit or delete anything. It does not access Gmail, Drive, contacts or any personal data of viewers.

How data is used and stored

Reports are saved as local files on the owner's own computer and are used only to plan the channel's videos. The data is not sold, shared or transferred to third parties and is not used for advertising.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Local reports can be deleted by the owner at any time.

Contact

Questions: contact the channel via its YouTube page, Still Built Right.
